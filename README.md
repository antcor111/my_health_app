# My Health App

A health tracking application to monitor your fitness journey by tracking weight and workouts.

## Features

- **Weight Tracking** - Log and monitor your weight over time
- **Workout Logging** - Record your workouts with details like exercise type, duration, and intensity
- **Progress Visualization** - View your health metrics and progress
